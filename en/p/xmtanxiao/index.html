What the series is

As mentioned on BlackBear EP195: this year I’m taking my kid to Johor Bahru, Malaysia for a month — the child goes to school, the grown-up looks around. Homework first, then school visits on the ground. “Xin–Ma School Visits” (新马探校, Singapore–Malaysia) is the note series that writes those visits up, from one parent’s point of view: which international schools there are around Johor Bahru’s EduCity, and what each one is like.

Published: 1/10 · LIS

Part 1 covers LINK International School (LIS) in EduCity, Johor Bahru, founded in 2023: roughly 70% local and 30% international students (mostly Chinese and Korean), taught entirely in English. Published on Xiaohongshu on 2026-08-07.

Up next

Planned follow-ups: Raffles American School, Tenby Schools Setia Eco Gardens and Marlborough College Malaysia. Links will be added here as each part goes out; order and count follow what actually gets published.

The prequel: BlackBear EP195

Before leaving I spent a whole episode with two born-and-raised Johor Bahru locals — what the city is like, how the cross-causeway commute works, enrolling a child in a nature school. Start there if you want to know Johor Bahru first.
