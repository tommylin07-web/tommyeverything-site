Why this exists

Since China’s 240-hour visa-free transit, more international travelers are landing in Xiamen — but the English internet has almost nothing good about the city: guides are outdated or read like translated brochures. SmartXiamen fills that gap with a local’s eye, then converts trust into paid signature experiences.

Five signature experiences

  • The XMU Insider Tour — into “China’s most beautiful campus” with a real student
  • Oyster Trail — from oyster-omelet stalls to the docks, eating your way into Xiamen’s sea
  • Tea Master Morning — a gongfu-tea morning with a tea master
  • Sound of Gulangyu — the piano island, by ear
  • Island at Dawn — see Kulangsu before the crowds do

The business loop

Content (guides plus a monthly This Month page) brings visitors from Google and Reddit → WhatsApp handles inquiries and bookings → a local guide network delivers. I don’t guide tours myself; I run the content, the trust and the dispatch.
