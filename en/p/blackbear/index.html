What the show is

BlackBear Podcast (This Amoiensis Life) launched in 2018, hosted by Tommy, biweekly. It doesn’t chase trends — it talks to the real people of Xiamen: founders, doctors, teachers, returnees. Eight years and 194 episodes later, it has become an oral archive of the city.

Content assets: a full transcript library

All 194 episodes have been transcribed with word-level timestamps through an automated pipeline. Every episode has its own page on the site with a readable, searchable transcript — eight years of conversations are no longer buried in audio, but usable text assets for search engines, AI and future projects.

The clip matrix: 8 posts a day, fully automated

  • Topic queue: AI reads the transcripts and picks the strongest moments
  • Programmatic rendering with Remotion: karaoke captions, bilingual subtitles, generated covers
  • Auto-published daily at 10:00 to WeChat Channels + YouTube, ~8 posts a day
  • A watchdog patrol plus iMessage briefings — failures page the human

Open source: PodClip Matrix

The pipeline behind all of this is now open source under MIT as PodClip Matrix: pick moments from word-level transcripts, render vertical clips and 3:4 covers with Remotion, then publish — one click or on schedule — to Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels, Douyin and YouTube, all from a zero-dependency local web console. It is agent-agnostic: anything that can run commands can drive it. Podcasters are welcome to self-host, file issues and build it together.
