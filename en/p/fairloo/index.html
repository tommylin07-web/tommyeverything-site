FairLoo

A civic mini-program where the public rates and ranks toilets — “Every stall deserves fairness.” Find one, review one, report a problem and see it fixed. Starting in Xiamen, pushing public restrooms toward compliance, fairness and inclusion.

My role Creator · dev & ops

2,245 toilet POIsFind → review → report → fix loopAI-generated weekly ops reports

Why this exists

Two overlooked problems: accessible “third restrooms” that stay locked and uncleaned, and a chronic shortage of women’s stalls — the national standard requires at least a 2:1 women-to-men ratio, rarely met. These “small things” affect the elderly, parents with kids, and every woman, every day. FairLoo turns public reviews into pressure for change.

The product loop

  • Find: geo-search for nearby toilets, 2,245 POIs imported
  • Review: rate cleanliness, accessibility, third-restroom availability — rankings follow
  • Report: locked doors, filth, broken facilities — one tap, with status tracked from pending to improving to resolved
  • Leaderboards and sharing: praise the good, chase the bad

How it runs

A WeChat mini-program on serverless CloudBase, operated under Xiamen Mujing Culture Media Co. Daily stats snapshots and weekly ops reports are generated automatically by AI — one person plus AI can keep a civic product alive and improving.
