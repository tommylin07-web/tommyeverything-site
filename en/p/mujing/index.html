Three product series

  • Meizhang Old Amoy series — the old city’s streets, buildings and memories as collectible goods
  • Animal-protection fridge magnets — co-branded with the local association, each one speaks for strays
  • Qixiao goods series — an original IP product line, in development

Why physical

Feeds scroll away; objects stay. Physical goods turn local storytelling into everyday things — a magnet on the fridge, a game on the table. The development runs human + AI too: topic research, artwork iteration and supplier comparison all involve the AI team.

The entity behind it

Operated by Xiamen Mujing Culture Media Co. (the same entity behind FairLoo). It shares a shelf with the Kulangsu Monopoly game: physical, local, cultural — one for the table, one for your hand.
