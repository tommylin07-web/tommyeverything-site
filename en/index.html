01 Trunk · ecosystem core Audio & content

BlackBear Podcast

The podcast · 194 eps · biweekly

An audio show first: a Xiamen-rooted podcast since 2018 — 194 episodes, biweekly, on the city, its people, education and culture. It is also the trunk of a content ecosystem; everything below grew out of the show.

Spin-off · Clip matrix · Short-video clips Each episode cut into vertical shorts — ~8 posts auto-published daily to WeChat Channels and YouTube.
Spin-off · Content assets · Transcript library All 194 episodes transcribed with word-level timestamps, readable and searchable on every episode page.
Spin-off · Open source · PodClip Matrix The whole clip pipeline, MIT-licensed: Remotion rendering, four-platform publishing and a local control panel — self-hostable by any podcaster.
Enter the trunk room → 02 Independent venture Local experiences

SmartXiamen

Live

An English-language insider site on Xiamen for international travelers: ten local-eye guides plus five signature experiences — Oyster Trail, Tea Master Morning, The XMU Insider Tour and more. Content brings visitors, WhatsApp takes bookings, local guides deliver: a complete small-business loop.

Enter → 03 Independent venture Global brand

Rowbuy

Brand in progress

A reverse cross-border shopping brand helping young shoppers in the West buy from China — collectibles, anime goods, electronics. The brand system is done: palette and type specs, mascot Captain Robi, an original theme song and three promo videos. Next up: the Discord community and a micro-KOL network.

Enter → 04 Independent venture Illustration publishing

New Amoyer

Trademark filed · recruiting illustrators

A Xiamen magazine that exists only in illustration — in the cover-art tradition of The New Yorker, following The Parisianer, The Tokyoiter and The Shanghairen. Illustrators paint the island city and share revenue instead of selling out their rights. Issue one: Kulangsu at dawn.

Enter → 05 Independent venture Physical goods

Kulangsu Monopoly

In design

An economic strategy board game set in Kulangsu’s International Settlement era (1903–1945): dual currencies, a hex map and real historical event cards. Every tile — Sunlight Rock, the Bagua Mansion, Shuzhuang Garden — carries the true story of a building and its people.

Enter → 06 Independent venture Physical goods

Mujing Goods

In development

The physical product line under Xiamen Mujing Culture Media — turning local memory into things you can hold. Three series in development: Meizhang Old Amoy, the animal-protection fridge-magnet series, and the Qixiao goods series. Same shelf as the Kulangsu board game: tangible, local, collectible.

Enter → 07 Independent venture Civic product

FairLoo

Live · civic

A civic mini-program where the public rates and ranks toilets — “Every stall deserves fairness.” Find one, review one, report a problem and see it fixed. Starting in Xiamen, pushing public restrooms toward compliance, fairness and inclusion.

Enter → 08 Independent venture Community revival

xmfish.com Revival

Proposal delivered

A three-stage revival proposal for xmfish.com, Xiamen’s 20-year-old community forum: stop the bleeding with static archives, monetize the content trove, then rebuild Reddit-style — each stage gated by validation. A city memory that once held five million members deserves a dignified restart.
